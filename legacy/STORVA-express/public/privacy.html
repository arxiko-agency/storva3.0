Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. What we collect

Account details (name, mobile number, email), store and product information you enter, order and customer details placed through your store, and technical data such as IP address and device type for security and analytics.

2. How we use it

To provide and secure the service, send one-time passwords, process orders and payments, send order notifications through WhatsApp, SMS or email, provide AI features you request, and meet legal obligations.

3. Customers of your store

For orders placed on a merchant’s store, the merchant is the controller of their customers’ data and we process it on their behalf. Customers should contact the merchant about their orders.

4. Service providers

We share only what is needed with providers that run the service: hosting and database (MongoDB Atlas, Render), payments (Razorpay), messaging (MSG91, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, email SMTP) and AI generation (Google Gemini). Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay and never stored by us.

5. Retention and security

Passwords are not used; OTP codes are stored only as hashes and expire within minutes. Data is encrypted in transit. We keep account data while your account is active and delete or anonymise it on request, except where law requires us to retain records such as invoices.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data by contacting us. You can withdraw consent for marketing messages at any time.

7. Cookies

We use a strictly necessary, httpOnly cookie to keep you signed in. Stores you build may add their own analytics tools at your choice.

Questions? Contact us at our support email.
